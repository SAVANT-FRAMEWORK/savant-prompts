# P41: Prompt Genealogy Architect

*Tier 9 — Recursive (premium research block) of the SAVANT FRAMEWORK operations prompt registry.*

P41 treats production prompts as an evolvable lineage rather than as fixed artifacts. Its end-state objective is prompts that improve themselves: useful variations are preserved, ineffective ones are discarded, and every lineage — living or extinct — is recorded with the reasons for its fate. The result is prompt engineering converted from artisanal intuition into a managed, auditable evolutionary process.

The methodology operates at the level of a discipline: a current best prompt is encoded as a 'genome'; a fitness function spanning accuracy, cost, and robustness is defined; a bounded population of variants is generated and selected against that fitness function in repeated generations; a human gate approves any promotion to production; and extinct lineages are archived with their failure rationales as a 'fossil record'. The deployment artifact set is an evolution engine, a fitness harness, and that fossil record. Population sizes, selection quotas, mutation operators, and generation budgets are part of the withheld prompt body and are not reproduced here.

Real-world scenario: a content-generation platform uses P41. The evolutionary process discovers that a reader-context framing instruction materially increases engagement — an insight no human on the team had thought to test.

## Methodology

Described at the methodological level only; the operational parameters that would reconstruct the prompt are withheld.
- **Genome encoding.** A prompt is represented as a structured, modifiable genome so that variation is systematic — specific components can be mutated, recombined, or held fixed — rather than produced by unconstrained paraphrase.
- **Multi-objective fitness.** Candidate prompts are scored on a defined fitness function covering at least accuracy, cost, and robustness, making 'better' an explicit, contestable claim rather than a vibe.
- **Gated evolution loop.** Variation and selection iterate over generations, with promotion to production gated by human approval. Automation proposes; accountable humans dispose.
- **Lineage memory.** Extinct variants are archived with the reasons for their extinction, so the system accumulates negative knowledge and does not re-explore known-dead regions of the design space.
- **Convergence awareness.** Improvement is tracked across generations so that a plateau is recognized as a signal — either to stop, or to change the fitness function or the search operators — rather than mistaken for continued progress.

## Merits

Reported optimization telemetry from the canonical registry:
- Prompt improvement of roughly 5% per week during the initial evolution phase.
- Human prompt-engineering time reduced by approximately 80%.
- Convergence detected when improvement falls below 1% over three generations.
The principal merits are the conversion of prompt engineering into a reproducible process with memory, and the surfacing of non-obvious prompt features (as in the reader-context framing in the scenario) that manual iteration rarely finds.

## Guidance for Use

Intended for teams operating prompts in production where incremental quality has measurable value. The fitness harness and evaluation data must exist before the evolution loop is switched on; evolving against a weak fitness function optimizes the metric, not the product.
The human approval gate is load-bearing. Do not remove it to accelerate iteration — it is what keeps cost, safety, and brand constraints inside the loop. The fossil record should be reviewed periodically; it is the cheapest source of prompt-design knowledge the system produces.
