# P37: Team Topology Optimizer

*Tier 8 — Strategic of the SAVANT FRAMEWORK operations prompt registry.*

P37 applies team-topology reasoning to organizational design with the end-state objective of teams aligned to business capabilities with minimal coordination overhead. It treats team structure as an architecture problem: boundaries, ownership, and interaction modes are designed deliberately and evolved on a schedule, rather than inherited from the last reorganization.

The reference deployment runs over five weeks: map business domains to bounded contexts; design team types and responsibilities; define interaction modes; plan the evolution path; and implement with quarterly review. The deployment artifact set is team charters, an ownership matrix, and an evolution roadmap.

Real-world scenario: a company uses P37 to reorganize from functional teams (frontend, backend, QA) to stream-aligned teams (onboarding, payments, content). Deployment frequency increases five-fold.

## Methodology

- **Domain-first mapping.** Business domains are mapped to bounded contexts before teams are drawn, so team boundaries follow the value streams they serve.
- **Typed teams.** Teams are designed against the four canonical types — stream-aligned, enabling, complicated-subsystem, platform — each with an explicit mission, rather than as undifferentiated pools of engineers.
- **Explicit interaction modes.** Inter-team relationships are declared as collaboration, X-as-a-service, or facilitation, with expected durations, replacing ambient coordination with designed coordination.
- **Ownership matrix.** Every capability has exactly one owning team; shared ownership is treated as unowned.
- **Scheduled evolution.** The topology is reviewed quarterly against friction signals (dependency load, handoff delays), on the premise that a static org chart in a changing business is technical debt in organizational form.

## Merits

Reported optimization telemetry from the canonical registry:
- Cross-team dependencies per feature held below two.
- Team autonomy score above 8/10.
- Time to market for a new capability under one month.
The principal merit is that coordination cost becomes visible and therefore manageable: dependencies are counted, owned, and designed away rather than absorbed as meeting load.

## Guidance for Use

Intended for engineering leadership and organizational designers. Reorganization is disruptive; use the friction telemetry (dependencies per feature, handoff time) to time changes, and prefer evolving boundaries over repeated wholesale re-orgs.
The ownership matrix must be enforced in practice — routing decisions, on-call, and budget — not merely published. A charter that reality ignores teaches the organization to ignore charters.
