# P42: Capability Emergence Trigger

*Tier 9 — Recursive (premium research block) of the SAVANT FRAMEWORK operations prompt registry.*

P42 addresses latent-capability activation: the end-state objective is capabilities that a model possesses in principle, activated in practice through progressive scaffolding. Rather than demanding a target behavior directly, the methodology decomposes it into prerequisites, establishes each prerequisite, and then sequences their composition until the target capability emerges — often as a discontinuous jump rather than a gradual improvement.

The deployment phases, at the methodological level: decompose the target capability into prerequisites; test each prerequisite independently; design a progressive activation sequence; detect emergence through a reproducible accuracy transition; then stabilize and generalize the capability. The deployment artifact set is a capability map, an activation sequence, and emergence tests. Real-world scenario: a legal AI uses P42 to activate implicit-causation detection. After progressive scaffolding, the model reliably understands unstated causal chains in contract language, improving contract-analysis quality by 25%.

## Methodology

Described at the methodological level only; operational detail is withheld.
- **Prerequisite decomposition.** The target capability is analyzed into the sub-capabilities it presupposes, on the premise that most 'missing' capabilities are missing foundations rather than missing potential.
- **Independent verification.** Each prerequisite is tested and established on its own before composition is attempted, so that failure at the composed level can be attributed to a specific missing foundation.
- **Progressive activation.** Prerequisites are composed in a designed sequence of increasing difficulty, allowing the model to build the target behavior on verified ground.
- **Emergence detection.** The transition to the target capability is measured as a reproducible performance discontinuity — not a lucky sample — with defined tests distinguishing genuine emergence from stochastic success.
- **Stabilization and generalization.** Once emerged, the capability is exercised on out-of-distribution cases to confirm it is a capability and not a memorized scaffold.

## Merits

Reported optimization telemetry from the source registry:
- Prerequisite accuracy above 90% required before the sequence advances.
- Emergence detection reproducible rather than stochastic.
- Generalization above 80% on out-of-distribution tests.
The principal merit is access to capability that direct instruction cannot reach: the legal-AI scenario's 25% improvement came from a capability the model had latently, surfaced by scaffolding rather than by a better demand.

## Guidance for Use

Intended for advanced prompt-engineering and applied-research teams. Budget evaluation effort generously; the methodology stands or falls on the quality of the prerequisite tests and the emergence detectors.
Treat any single-sequence success as a hypothesis. Confirm with the emergence tests and out-of-distribution checks before depending on the capability in production, particularly in legal, clinical, or financial contexts.
