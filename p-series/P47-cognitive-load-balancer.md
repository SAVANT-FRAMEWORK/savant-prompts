# P47: Cognitive Load Balancer

*Tier 10 — Orchestration (premium research block) of the SAVANT FRAMEWORK operations prompt registry.*

P47 governs the human-AI division of labor with the end-state objective of optimal collaboration in which humans remain skilled and engaged. It treats automation complacency and skill decay as first-class failure modes: a system that removes all difficulty from its human operators also removes their ability to intervene when the system fails.

The deployment steps, at the methodological level: assess cognitive load in real time for both human and AI; dynamically shift responsibility between them; maintain human skill through a mandated fraction of manual practice; enable graceful handoffs in both directions; and actively prevent automation complacency. The deployment artifact set is a load balancer, a handoff protocol, and an engagement tracker. The load-estimation signals, shifting policy, and handoff choreography that would reconstruct the prompt are withheld.

Real-world scenario: an air-traffic-control system uses P47. Controllers retain 20% manual handling, maintaining the skills needed for emergencies, while the AI absorbs routine work and reduces overall workload by 40%.

## Methodology

Described at the methodological level only; operational detail is withheld.
- **Real-time load assessment.** Cognitive load is estimated continuously for the human operator and the AI system, so allocation decisions respond to current state rather than to a static policy.
- **Dynamic responsibility shifting.** Tasks are reallocated between human and AI as load changes — offloading an overloaded human, or re-engaging an under-loaded one before vigilance decays.
- **Deliberate skill maintenance.** A fixed share of work (20% in the reference deployment) is kept manual, not because automation cannot do it, but because the human's retained skill is the contingency plan for the day automation cannot.
- **Graceful handoffs.** Transfer of control in either direction follows a defined protocol with confirmation, so neither party ever discovers by accident that it is — or is not — in charge.
- **Complacency countermeasures.** Engagement is tracked and actively maintained, treating 'the operator stopped watching' as a system defect rather than an operator defect.

## Merits

Reported optimization telemetry from the canonical registry:
- Human engagement above 70% active monitoring.
- Handoff time under two seconds.
- Skill maintenance verified quarterly.
The principal merit is resilience: total workload falls (40% in the scenario) while the human-in-the-loop remains a genuine fallback rather than a ceremonial one.

## Guidance for Use

Intended for teams automating high-consequence operations — aviation, clinical monitoring, industrial control, security operations. The manual-practice fraction is a safety control; do not optimize it away as inefficiency, and verify retained skill on a schedule.
Handoff events are the highest-risk moments of any shared-control system. Rehearse them, instrument them, and treat every handoff failure as an incident worthy of review.
