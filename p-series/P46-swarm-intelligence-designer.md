# P46: Swarm Intelligence Designer

*Tier 10 — Orchestration (premium research block) of the SAVANT FRAMEWORK operations prompt registry.*

P46 designs multi-agent collectives with the end-state objective of emergent intelligence from simple agents, solving problems no individual agent can. The design philosophy is deliberately bottom-up: simple local rules, coordination through a shared environment, and convergence mechanisms produce collective capability that was never explicitly programmed into any member.

The deployment phases, at the methodological level: define simple, local agent rules with no global knowledge; design a shared environment supporting stigmergic coordination (agents coordinate through traces left in the environment, as in natural swarms); implement opinion dynamics with convergence detection; add quality assurance against groupthink; then scale and monitor. The deployment artifact set is a swarm specification, an environment design, and a convergence algorithm. The rule templates, environment schema, and convergence-detection machinery that would reconstruct the prompt are withheld.

Real-world scenario: a drug-discovery platform uses P46. Swarms of fifty agents explore molecular space and surface novel candidate compounds three times faster than individual researchers.

## Methodology

Described at the methodological level only; operational detail is withheld.
- **Simple local rules.** Agents are given deliberately simple, local behaviors — no agent holds global knowledge — because robust emergence comes from interaction, not from individual sophistication.
- **Stigmergic environment.** Coordination happens through a shared environment in which agents leave and react to traces, decoupling agents in time and avoiding fragile point-to-point coordination.
- **Opinion dynamics with convergence detection.** The collective's beliefs evolve under defined dynamics, and convergence is detected explicitly so the swarm knows when it has an answer.
- **Groupthink countermeasures.** Quality-assurance mechanisms preserve diversity of exploration and punish premature consensus, guarding against the failure mode in which the swarm converges confidently on a mediocre solution.
- **Instrumented scaling.** The swarm is scaled with monitoring of collective accuracy, convergence time, and groupthink rate, so growth is governed by evidence rather than enthusiasm.

## Merits

Reported optimization telemetry from the source registry:
- Collective accuracy more than 15% above the best individual agent.
- Convergence within five rounds.
- Groupthink rate below 5%.
The principal merit is parallel exploration with a wisdom-of-collectives dividend: the swarm is both faster than its members and better than its best member, which sequential or single-agent designs cannot achieve.

## Guidance for Use

Intended for research and platform teams exploring combinatorial or search-heavy problem spaces — discovery, optimization, design. Start with a small swarm and validate the collective-accuracy premium before scaling; a swarm that does not beat its best member is overhead, not intelligence.
Watch the groupthink metric continuously. Premature convergence is the characteristic failure mode of collective systems, and the countermeasures only work if someone is reading the gauge.
