# P40: Strategic Roadmap Synthesizer

*Tier 8 — Strategic of the SAVANT FRAMEWORK operations prompt registry.*

P40 synthesizes technical strategy with the end-state objective that every technical initiative is mapped directly to a business outcome, with clear sequencing. It combines Wardley mapping, build/buy/partner analysis, and dependency-aware sequencing into a roadmap that leadership can read and engineers can execute.

The reference deployment runs over five weeks: map capabilities on a Wardley map; make build/buy/partner decisions; sequence initiatives by dependency and value; define success metrics; and build optionality into each decision. The deployment artifact set is a Wardley map, initiative cards, and a dependency graph.

Real-world scenario: a company uses P40 to plan a two-year technical strategy. When the market shifts, the company pivots in three weeks instead of six months, because each initiative was structured to preserve optionality.

## Methodology

- **Situational mapping.** Capabilities are positioned on a Wardley map (value chain against evolution stage), exposing which components differentiate and which are commodities — the factual basis for every subsequent decision.
- **Sourcing decisions.** Build, buy, or partner is decided per component from the map: differentiation is built, commodities are bought, and uncertainty is partnered around.
- **Dependency-and-value sequencing.** Initiatives are ordered by what unblocks what and by value delivered per unit of time, producing a sequence that is defensible initiative by initiative.
- **Success metrics per initiative.** Every initiative card carries the business metric it exists to move, enabling both prioritization and honest post-hoc evaluation.
- **Optionality engineering.** Each decision is structured to keep future choices open — modular boundaries, reversible commitments, staged investments — so the roadmap can bend without breaking when the environment changes.

## Merits

Reported optimization telemetry from the canonical registry:
- Initiative business-case clarity above 90% — leadership understands what each initiative is for.
- On-time delivery above 80%.
- Strategic pivot cost held below two weeks because optionality is preserved.
The principal merit is alignment that survives scrutiny: the roadmap reads the same way to the board, to engineering, and to the teams executing it, because each initiative traces to a mapped capability and a named metric.

## Guidance for Use

Intended for CTOs, VPs of Engineering, and technical strategy functions. Refresh the Wardley map at least twice a year; component evolution is continuous, and a stale map produces confidently wrong sourcing decisions.
Resist compressing the optionality work under delivery pressure. The three-week pivot in the scenario is purchased entirely by the modular boundaries and reversible commitments made earlier.
