# S-015: Adversarial Epistemology and Information Warfare Defense

**Activation Context:** When truth-seeking itself is under attack by sophisticated actors using disinformation, epistemic pollution, or narrative manipulation.

**Prompt:**
> Operate as an adversarial epistemologist and information warfare defense architect. Your task is to design *epistemic immune systems* — institutional and cognitive defenses against sophisticated attacks on the capacity to know.
>
> 1. **Attack Surface Mapping**: Classify epistemic attacks: (a) *flooding* (overwhelming signal with noise), (b) *fragmentation* (breaking consensus on basic facts), (c) *credentialing attacks* (corrupting expertise signals), (d) *context collapse* (stripping information of provenance and intent), (e) *emotional hijacking* (triggering affective responses that override reasoning), and (f) *recursive doubt* (attacking the possibility of knowledge itself).
> 2. **Trust Architecture**: Design a *distributed trust system* that does not rely on any single authority. Include: (a) multi-source verification with independence requirements, (b) provenance chains with cryptographic integrity, (c) adversarial diversity (ensuring critics have incentives and platforms), and (d) reputation systems resistant to Sybil attacks and brigading.
> 3. **Cognitive Inoculation**: Design *prebunking* and *inoculation* protocols: (a) exposing individuals to weakened forms of manipulation techniques to build resistance, (b) teaching *lateral reading* (checking sources before engaging content), and (c) fostering *epistemic humility* without collapsing into relativism.
> 4. **Institutional Resilience**: Specify organizational structures that resist epistemic capture: (a) *red teams* with formal authority and protection, (b) *whistleblower architectures* with genuine anonymity, (c) *cross-institutional verification* (competing organizations fact-checking each other), and (d) *sunset clauses* on accepted knowledge (forcing periodic re-verification).
> 5. **Signal Recovery Under Pollution**: Design algorithms and heuristics for extracting true signal from heavily polluted information environments. Include: (a) *adversarial filtering* (identifying content designed to manipulate), (b) *consilience mapping* (finding agreement across ideologically diverse sources), and (c) *prediction market integration* (using economic incentives to surface true beliefs).
> 6. **Metacognitive Defenses**: Train the *second-order ability* to recognize when one's own reasoning has been compromised. Include: (a) emotional state monitoring, (b) source bias auditing, (c) temporal consistency checks (does this belief cohere with what I believed before exposure to new information?), and (d) social proof resistance.
> 7. Conclude with an *epistemic resilience metric*: a quantitative measure of an institution's or individual's resistance to epistemic attack, with diagnostic tests.

**Deeper Abstraction:** Social epistemology under adversarial conditions — philosophy of knowledge meets information security.
