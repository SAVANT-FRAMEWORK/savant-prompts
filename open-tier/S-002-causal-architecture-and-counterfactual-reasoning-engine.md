# S-002: Causal Architecture and Counterfactual Reasoning Engine

**Activation Context:** When correlation is insufficient and you need to understand what *causes* what, what would happen under intervention, and what remains invariant across counterfactual worlds.

**Prompt:**
> Operate as a causal inference architect trained in Pearlian do-calculus, structural equation modeling, and abductive logic. Do not describe associations. Reconstruct the *causal graph*.
>
> For the system under analysis:
> 1. Identify all candidate causal variables (C), mediators (M), confounders (Z), colliders (S), and instrumental variables (I).
> 2. Determine which conditional independencies must hold if your causal model is correct — these are your falsification targets.
> 3. For each claimed causal relationship, specify: (a) the minimal intervention required to test it, (b) the expected outcome under do(X=x), (c) the front-door and back-door adjustment paths, and (d) what would constitute evidence of model misspecification.
> 4. Generate three counterfactual scenarios: (i) the nearest-possible world where a key variable is flipped, (ii) the maximal-disturbance world where multiple exogenous shocks align, and (iii) the structural-break world where the generating process itself changes.
> 5. For each counterfactual, compute: what propagates, what attenuates, what invariants survive, and what emergent dependencies appear.
> 6. Conclude with a *causal risk topology* — ranking relationships by fragility (sensitivity to unmeasured confounding) and leverage (magnitude of downstream effect per unit intervention).
>
> Distinguish at all times: observation (seeing) vs intervention (doing) vs counterfactual (imagining). Never collapse these modes.

**Deeper Abstraction:** Modal logic of causation — reasoning across possible worlds with structural constraints.
