# S-008: Abductive Diagnostic Mastery Protocol

**Activation Context:** When facing complex problems with incomplete information, where deduction is impossible and induction is insufficient.

**Prompt:**
> Operate in abductive diagnostic mode — inference to the best explanation under radical uncertainty. You are not proving; you are hypothesizing, weighing, and eliminating.
>
> 1. **Symptom Clustering**: Group observed phenomena into *syndromes* — patterns that co-occur more often than chance would predict. Do not assume independent causes for correlated symptoms.
> 2. **Hypothesis Generation**: Apply *inference to the best explanation* (IBE). For each syndrome, generate the full set of plausible explanations, including: (a) common causes, (b) causal chains, (c) emergent interactions, and (d) hidden variables. Do not prune prematurely.
> 3. **Explanatory Power Ranking**: For each hypothesis H, evaluate: (a) *scope* — how many symptoms does it explain? (b) *simplicity* — how many auxiliary assumptions does it require? (c) *independence* — how well does it predict unobserved symptoms? (d) *coherence* — how well does it integrate with established knowledge?
> 4. **Differential Diagnosis**: For the top two hypotheses, design the *crucial experiment* — the observation or intervention that would maximally discriminate between them. Specify what result would falsify each.
> 5. **Base Rate Integration**: Explicitly incorporate prior probabilities. If base rates are unknown, specify the sensitivity of your conclusion to different base rate assumptions.
> 6. **Error Mode Analysis**: Identify the most likely diagnostic failure modes: (a) *availability bias* — over-weighting memorable explanations, (b) *confirmation bias* — seeking only confirming evidence, (c) *premature closure* — settling on an explanation before exhausting alternatives, and (d) *fundamental attribution* — over-weighting dispositional vs. situational causes.
> 7. Conclude with a *diagnostic confidence lattice*: not a single answer, but a ranked set of explanations with their relative likelihoods, key differentiating tests, and conditions under which you would revise the ranking.

**Deeper Abstraction:** Philosophy of scientific discovery — Peircean abduction formalized for operational deployment.
