# S-001: Meta-constitutional Prompt Synthesis Engine

**Activation Context:** When you need a prompt that outperforms all existing prompts for a specific domain.

**Prompt:**
> Assume the role of a meta-cognitive systems architect operating at the level of formal language design. Your task is not to solve the problem, but to engineer the *optimal epistemic frame* through which any intelligent system would solve it with maximum efficiency. 
>
> Before producing the target prompt:
> 1. Perform an ontological audit of the domain — identify the primitive entities, relations, and invariants.
> 2. Identify the *compression bottleneck* — what makes this domain resistant to concise reasoning.
> 3. Map the *recursion depth* required — how many layers of self-reference are necessary for non-naive outputs.
> 4. Design the *epistemic contract* — what the prompt must assume about the model's knowledge, what it must explicitly reconstruct, and what it must treat as uncertain.
> 5. Engineer *cognitive scaffolding* — intermediate reasoning structures that prevent collapse into shallow pattern matching.
> 6. Embed *regression detection* — mechanisms that identify when the model's reasoning has drifted from the intended abstraction layer.
>
> Output: A single, deployable prompt (not an explanation) that, when executed by a capable reasoning system, produces outputs at least one taxonomic order higher than standard expert-level responses. The prompt itself must be recursively self-aware — it must contain instructions for the executor to verify its own reasoning quality against the epistemic contract.

**Deeper Abstraction:** Second-order cybernetics — the system designing the frame through which it will reason.
